Business units

Six specialised units, one supply chain

Backward linkage from our own spinning means yarn, fabric and finish are planned together, so quality and lead times are ours to control.

Spinning

Cotton, viscose and blended yarns from 6 to 100 count on 93,000 spindles at Hazrat Amanat Shah Spinning Mills, feeding our woven and knit programmes.

Weaving

Picanol rapier and air-jet looms from Belgium, running up to 1,000 rpm for around 60,000 metres a day.

Yarn dyeing

European dyeing machinery and proven dyestuffs, with a biological effluent plant treating every litre of process water.

Solid dyeing

A computerised colour kitchen and automatic dosing keep shades consistent from lab dip to bulk.

Printing

Italian rotary screen printing with up to 16 colours in a single pass, for anything from subtle prints to bold all-overs.

Finishing

Peach, soft, regular, wrinkle-free and easy-care finishes to international standards, tuned for look, performance and hand feel.
