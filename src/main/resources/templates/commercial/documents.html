Export PIs

Offered to the buyer from marketing's delivery schedules, at the schedule's price - with the advising bank, tenure, INCO terms and the fabric's weight. The buyer's LC received against PIs: its numbers and banks, UDs and UPs, the raw-material LCs opened back-to-back on it, and cash incentive. Invoiced under an LC from the delivery challans (or in advance), then realized through the bank, step by step, to final payment. A supplier's PI for what a requisition needs (or items directly): checked off, opened on an LC, and ordered as an import. The LC (or TT) opened on import PIs: bank, port, bill of entry and costs - which the goods take into stock as landed cost.
