Purchase orders

A department asks its store for items. Approved, it is issued from stock or bought through a purchase requisition. What the store needs bought - from a store requisition, or raised directly. Approved, purchase orders are placed against it. Items ordered from a supplier at an agreed price - Direct, Spot or Import. Approved, the store receives against it with an MRR. Goods received from a supplier against a purchase order, into a store, at the order's price. Posted by the store. Goods sent back to the supplier against an MRR, out of the store they were received into. Posted by the store. Items handed out of a store against a store requisition, or directly. Valued at the store's average cost. Posted by the store. Items into a store without a purchase order - opening stock, returns from the floor. Posted by the store. One store asks another for items. Approved, the sending store issues against it. Items out of one store on their way to another. They are in transit until the receiving store receives them. The receiving store takes in what a transfer issue sent, at the cost it left at. A physical count's differences: stock added or taken away, with a reason. Written to stock when approved. Fabric lots out of one store on their way to another - greige to the processing store, finished fabric to the delivering store. The receiving store takes in the fabric a transfer issue sent, lot for lot.
