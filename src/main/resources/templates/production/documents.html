Production orders

One per fabric type, raised from an approved Booking. The route decides the work orders it needs. Greige to weave against a production order - per fabric line, or per colour when the colour is in the yarn. Dye, print, finish or rework against a production order's colours. Greige is issued to it and finished fabric received from it. Greige off the loom, against a Weaving WO, into a greige store. Posted by the store. Greige handed to a dye batch, against a Dyeing WO, out of a greige store. Posted by the store. Finished fabric from the dye house by dye lot, shade and grade, into a finished store. Posted by the store. Marketing's delivery dates and quantities against production order lines - what the store may deliver. One dispatch against an approved schedule; approval checks the buyer's credit and reserves the lots. The challan and gate pass: fabric out of the store against a delivery order. Posted by the store.
