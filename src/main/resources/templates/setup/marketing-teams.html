Marketing teams

Each team sees only its own bookings and production orders, and can have its own approval matrix.

New team

Give it a code and a name; add its people once it is saved.

The leader is chosen from the team's members. Incentive is not set here: it is paid on realised proceeds, in Commercial.

A member sees this team's bookings and production orders and no other team's, and every booking they raise is filed under this team. A person is in one team at a time.

This team's bookings are approved under its own approval matrix when it has one - its levels, roles, named people and amount bands - and under the business unit's matrix when it has none. A document is decided under the matrix it was submitted with.
